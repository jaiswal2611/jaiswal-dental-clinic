Treatments in detail

What each treatment is for, and what usually happens, step by step. Timing and cost depend on your case and are explained at your consultation.

Dental implants

A permanent solution for missing teeth, planned around the health of your bone and gums.

  • One missing tooth
  • Several missing teeth
  1. Examination and X-rayWe check your teeth, gums and bone to see whether an implant is suitable.
  2. PlanningDr. Jaiswal plans the placement and explains the options, timing and cost.
  3. Placement and healingThe implant is placed carefully, and the area is given time to heal.
  4. Crown fittingA crown is fitted on the implant so it looks and works like a natural tooth.

Orthodontic treatment

Braces that perfect your smile and build confidence, including preparation before jaw surgery (presurgical orthodontics).

  • Crooked teeth
  • Gaps
  • Bite problems
  1. Records and planWe examine your teeth and bite, then plan how they will move.
  2. Braces fittedBraces are placed and you learn how to keep them clean.
  3. Regular adjustmentsVisits to adjust the braces and check your progress.
  4. RetentionAfter the braces come off, retainers help hold your new smile in place.

Smile designing

Refining the shape, colour and balance of your smile with tooth-coloured fillings and careful cosmetic work.

  • Uneven teeth
  • Chipped teeth
  • Small gaps
  1. Smile consultationYou tell us what you would like to change.
  2. PlanWe suggest realistic options for your teeth.
  3. TreatmentCosmetic work is done in comfortable steps.
  4. Finishing and reviewWe polish, check the result and show you how to care for it.

Cleaning and whitening

A healthier, brighter smile, from routine scaling and polishing to whitening.

  • Stains
  • Tartar build-up
  • Dull teeth
  1. ExaminationWe check your gums and teeth first.
  2. Scaling and polishingBuild-up is removed and your teeth are polished.
  3. Whitening, if suitableSuitable teeth can be brightened.
  4. Home careWe show you how to keep the result fresh.

Root canal treatment

Pain relief that saves your natural tooth.

  • Toothache
  • Deep decay
  • Swelling near a tooth
  1. Examination and X-rayWe find the cause of the pain.
  2. Numbing and cleaningThe area is numbed, and the infected tissue inside the tooth is cleaned out.
  3. SealingThe inside of the tooth is sealed.
  4. Final filling or crownThe tooth is rebuilt so you can chew on it again.

Crowns and bridges

Restoring strength and function to damaged or missing teeth. Dentures and surgical extraction, including wisdom teeth, are also available.

  • Broken teeth
  • Weak teeth
  • Gaps from missing teeth
  1. ExaminationWe check how much healthy tooth is left.
  2. PreparationThe tooth is shaped and an impression is taken.
  3. Temporary and lab workA temporary may be fitted while the final one is made.
  4. FittingThe crown or bridge is fitted, checked and adjusted.
